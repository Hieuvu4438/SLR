# Causal failure map — reconstruction, not method selection

2026-09-22. Evidence refers to current source and surviving historical records.
“Unresolved” means an unanswered causal question, not an invitation to rerun a
closed method. New measurements are limited to the TRAIN census and evidence
hash audit in Cycle 1.

| Layer | Evidence | What it does not establish | Permissible next question |
|---|---|---|---|
| A Input information | Historical exact-input collapse tests found unique inputs; raw pose/RGB resources differ between recipes. | Uniqueness does not show preservation of handshape, mouth or spatial grammar. | Is a particular missing cue independently annotated and recoverable under a fair resource control? |
