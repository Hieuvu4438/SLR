Layers use proposal9's A–M definitions, not the historical table's older letter
mapping. Status OPEN refers to a scientific question, never permission to
reopen a closed method. REJECTED applies to the bounded proposed explanatory
route, not every conceivable theory. No question here is a SUPPORTED-FOR-PILOT
method. Nearest priors identify collision targets, not a claim that each paper
answers the question. Detailed primary-source verification remains in progress.

Priority vectors are subjective **question-investigation** scores in order:
evidence / information gain / retrieval relevance / novelty opportunity /
falsifiability / feasibility / distance from closed families; maxima
20/20/20/15/10/10/5. They are not probabilities or measurements. A high score
cannot override a fatal collision or missing experiment. Numeric ranking is
subordinate to the stated decision consequence.
