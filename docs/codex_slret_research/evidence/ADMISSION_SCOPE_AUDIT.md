requires six conditions for SUPPORTED-FOR-PILOT: an observed or strongly
indicated bottleneck; a falsifiable hypothesis; material distinction from closed

## Corrected evidence boundaries

| Claim or action | Evidence sufficient for that scope | What does not follow |
|---|---|---|
| Official-pair rank, margin, optimization or numerical diagnostic | Existing split/ID/relevance contract, reproducible computation and a predefined hypothesis/control | Unpaired videos are linguistically irrelevant |
| A computational/statistical bottleneck motivates a pilot | Observed or strongly indicated signature plus a falsifiable core-mechanism test, and all other section41 gates | Generic errors alone justify another module |
| Confuser preserves meaning, loses mouthing information, or changes a simultaneous articulator relation | Compatible independent evidence supporting that particular video-semantic claim | Gloss/text disagreement or a model's answer supplies video truth |
| Mechanism discovery on CSL/H2 | In-scope permitted resources, official protocol, explicit exploratory status and normal admission rules | A post-hoc dataset switch confirms a failed PH hypothesis |
| Cross-dataset confirmation | Fixed predictions and controls, compatible second-dataset protocol, disclosed differences | Retuning on each dataset is an independent confirmation |

An intervention effect on official pairing may be meaningful without proving a
particular linguistic explanation. Conclusions must say which estimand was
tested. Synthetic numerical controls can check algebra or implementation, but
cannot establish natural video semantics. Existing expert annotations may be
used only within their documented scope; no reviewer, review packet or generated
semantic labels are needed or authorized here.

The strongest counterargument is that relaxing a semantic gate could revive
arbitrary architecture search. It does not: the six original gates, local
closures, matched controls and deployment evaluation remain binding. A
computational hypothesis still needs its own evidence. Lack of semantic labels
is neither evidence for it nor a reason to close all research.
