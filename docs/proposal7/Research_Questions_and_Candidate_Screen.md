## Methodology Blueprint

Pragmatic quantitative, exploratory secondary-data/code audit followed by controlled falsification, not a systematic review or clinical study. Use all existing PH dev queries and the complete fixed gallery; descriptive train-fitted strata; paired source-cluster bootstrap with 10,000 draws. Generalization unit is a training run plus dataset, not 519 independent training replications. Do not pool paper recalls as a meta-analysis across different resources/galleries. Confirmatory pilots require a timestamped frozen protocol; no preregistration has been submitted externally.

Internal validity: identical initialization, RNG order, updates, inputs and selection metric across arms. External validity: three sign languages separately, with PH exploration acknowledged. Construct validity: caption equality is not sign equivalence; a filename source prefix is not a visual-duplicate certificate. Reliability: exact hashes, rank fixtures, source pinning. No claim of causal identification from a correlation or a module's name.

No new participants, identity recognition, crowdsourcing or video publication. Existing human video remains under its dataset terms; institutional determination of secondary-use/IRB status is required before a human study or new annotation campaign. No exemption is self-certified. Reporting standard is reproducible ML benchmarking with a search log, not PRISMA/CONSORT compliance. No cross-model review or human sign-language validation has occurred.

## Cycle 1: candidate mechanisms

Four **mutually different** interventions are considered; consideration is not an assertion that they pass internal/external novelty. They change respectively the text information channel, the visual measurement representation, information acquisition, and the probabilistic scoring model. Shared use of a baseline is not their proposed novelty.

### A — Native-caption information preservation

Hypothesis: a lossy native→English channel removes distinctions required for ranking. Retain native and English input jointly with a train-only bilingual encoder and ordinary full-caption retrieval loss; gradients go from full-gallery-compatible contrastive scores into the text mapping. Video stream and scoring are otherwise fixed. Inference changes text encoding and cached text vectors; approximately one additional text pass, measured rather than assumed parameter-matched.

Evidence: actual duplicate-caption failure stratum, but translation-specific collision count is pending. Closest SLRet: CiCo's translation channel versus C²RL/SAN native-language encoders. Adjacent multilingual multimodal retrieval must be checked. Distinction from ELSC/DIVE/PLEL/SSSC: no lexical margin, mined support or caption substitution. From OCEM/sampling: no transport/capacity. From PMGR: no group-risk correction. From RPCA: no generation or protected gradients. Fatal novelty risk: this may be ordinary multilingual encoding; a second language/encoder is not by itself a contribution. Falsify with the exact-collapse ceiling and native-only/English-only/equal-capacity controls. Native sentence text must never be treated as literal sign-language word order.

### B — Articulation via inverse graphics

Hypothesis: 2D/pooled appearance conflates distinct hand articulations. Fit a differentiable hand geometry model from permitted existing raw frames/pose, then train a sentence encoder over recovered articulatory trajectories. Retrieval gradients would reach pose/shape inference; reconstruction terms constrain geometry. Inference requires geometry extraction/caching, potentially substantial new preprocessing.

Evidence: no verified hand-articulation cause in saved errors; SEDS and pose pretraining show representation relevance but do not identify this bottleneck. Closest SLRet SEDS; adjacent 3D hand reconstruction/sign recognition must be checked. Unlike the local-score families, the intervention changes the measured visual variable before alignment. Unlike PMGR/RPCA it changes neither population risk nor optimizer protection. Fatal risks: collapses into closed generic extra-stream/backbone/pose improvement; monocular geometry may hallucinate occluded fingers, discard facial grammar, or cost more without rank gain. Kill on equal-compute crop/2D controls or absent information-specific gain. No externally pretrained asset assumed available.

### C — Budgeted active visual acquisition

Hypothesis: a small subset of ambiguous queries needs high-resolution raw evidence that the stored I3D features cannot supply. A policy decides whether/where to acquire another crop/frame, paying an explicit compute cost, then reranks. The distinctive intended path is **acquisition of an unavailable measurement**, not reweighting existing tokens. Policy gradients or a differentiable expected-cost surrogate would trade retrieval loss against decoding/encoder calls. Inference is query-dependent and more expensive; entire-gallery first-stage rankings remain fixed and shortlist coverage bounds recall.

Evidence: recurring errors/top-K coverage remain to be measured; no proof missing raw detail causes them. Closest SLRet: CiCo's dense sampling; adjacent adaptive video sampling must be checked. No support-margin or OT objective, but this can still collide with DIVE/local reranking, BMR/temporal ensembles and reliability gates if it merely selects existing evidence. Kill if low-cost fixed sampling or compute-matched uniform crops match it; also kill if acquisition cannot exceed the frozen-feature control. It cannot claim all-gallery efficiency by hiding decoder/crop cost.

### D — Conditional visual likelihood-ratio retrieval

Hypothesis: similarity scores confuse generic visual dynamics with query-specific evidence. Fit a conditional density of visual feature sequences given a sentence and an unconditional visual density, then rank by log p(features|text) − log p(features). A conditional flow or diffusion density surrogate would send full-sequence likelihood gradients into the text conditioner and visual density, rather than a local matching matrix. Inference scores every candidate pair, or uses a disclosed shortlist; substantially greater compute than cached dot products.

Evidence: no current measurement establishes a density-estimation bottleneck. Closest SLRet: UPRet distributional embeddings, not raw conditional visual likelihood. Adjacent generative retrieval/density-ratio models must be checked. Unlike RPCA, no sentence decoder or gradient protection; unlike OCEM, no transport; unlike PMGR, no population correction. However nuisance subtraction/uncertainty can collide with the conceptual closures. Fatal risks: likelihood models nuisance detail, approximated likelihood is uncalibrated, or gains are only extra capacity. Kill with unconditional-only, shuffled-conditioning and equal-compute discriminative controls. No assumption that spoken captions specify all sign-video details.

## Cycle 1: collision search and four-perspective review

Executed mechanism searches after defining A–D. Primary sources establish that multilingual parameter sharing is already used by [mTVR/mXML](https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.00061) (abstract); differentiable MANO intermediates with recognition and geometric consistency by [Hand-Model-Aware SLR](https://cdn.aaai.org/ojs/16247/16247-13-19741-1-2-20210518.pdf) (Our Approach, read through objectives); a learned compute-saving frame policy by [mmSampler](https://proceedings.mlsys.org/paper_files/paper/2022/hash/d59a1dc497cf2773637256f50f492723-Abstract.html) (abstract); and generative diffusion-based video retrieval by [DiffusionRet](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.09867) (abstract and introduction from official ICCV PDF). These are mechanism precedents, **not** proof of identical equations or fair SLRet performance.

## Cycle 2: targeted diagnostics and redesign

Following the failed first screen, narrow the search to two salvageable questions, not new names for old losses: (i) is information lost *before* matching, and (ii) can a genuinely different conditional-density score be justified rather than another similarity function? The additional asset-free diagnostic is `tools/diagnose_information_channels.py`; it compares native strings with the exact 30-content-token input partition, validates saved score identity and measures seed-persistent/top-K errors. This is additional to the first cycle's length/rarity/duplicate/source-bootstrap and historical-gate analyses. Both cycles are exploratory, not independently preregistered studies.

[M] PH dev has 509 unique native strings and 507 model inputs; two mixed-native collision classes contain seven queries. Duplicate-input score columns are bit-identical in all three runs. In seed 42 these seven account for only 5.22% of T2V and 5.69% of V2T errors. Even an impossible perfect slice-only fix adds at most 1.349 pp per direction, not proof of an achievable gain. H1's ≥10% dominant-bottleneck claim fails; semantic mistranslation outside exact collisions is unmeasured.

[M] The three seeds share 92 T2V and 87 V2T errors, 68.66%/70.73% of seed-42 errors. Of these, 70/63 already lie in seed-42 top 10. H2's stable-error criterion holds. A per-query answer-aware seed oracle reaches 82.274%/83.237%; it is **not deployable**, not an ensemble result, and does not prove additional raw information is necessary. H3 remains untested, not disproven.

Redesign A2: eliminate the extra English branch and test native-only encoding as an information-channel control. This is worthwhile baseline work but still not a novel method; no resurrection as Proposal 7.

Redesign C2: acquire fixed high-resolution information for every video first, removing the policy and local support selection. Test raw-versus-frozen information before designing any acquisition algorithm. This becomes a **diagnostic experiment**, not a candidate architecture or a scale-only SOTA claim.

Redesign D2: require a conditional density whose ranking cannot be algebraically reduced to the baseline and whose benefit survives unconditional/shuffled-conditioning controls. Additional density-ratio collision search and an analytic reduction check were completed. A different density family without evidence would not suffice.

Second-cycle result: [CPC §2.2–2.3](https://arxiv.org/html/1807.03748v2) already motivates density-ratio contrastive scores; [Ren et al.](https://papers.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2019/hash/1e79596878b2320cac26dd792a6c51c9-Abstract.html) already addresses background-sensitive likelihoods in OOD detection. Neither paper proves D2 identical, but the proposed novelty slogans are not new. Analytically, isotropic equal-variance Gaussian log p(z|t) − log p(z), with conditional mean μ(t), equals zᵀμ(t)/σ² − ||μ(t)||²/(2σ²). For unit-norm means it is ordinary dot-product ranking in both directions. Also, subtracting log p(z) alone is constant across candidate texts for one video, so cannot change V2T ordering. The four synthetic tests in `test_information_channels.py` pass, including both reductions; they do not establish learned-model efficacy.

Second four-perspective assessment: A2 is a useful nonnovel text baseline; C2 is a necessary information diagnostic rather than a method; D2 has no new evidence that justifies its cost or resolves the ranking objection. Linguistic and reproducibility objections remain. Scores after redesign A2=51, C2=40, D2=28 are not upgrades to GO, since none is a defensible novel method. **NO-GO for selecting a new SOTA method after these two cycles.** Highest-value unresolved experiment: an equal-resource crossed raw-information/frozen-feature probe (specified in the final report), not another support/OT/generation-loss training campaign. This is a decision about the proposals considered under current evidence, not an impossibility theorem for SLRet.
